Mathematical Equalization: Pro-Rata Allocation ($P_{\text{fair}}$).

Every participant pays the identical cleared price:
$P = \frac{\Delta \text{SOL}_{\text{committed}}}{\text{Supply}_{\text{launch}}}$

Deposit 1 SOL or 10 SOL, your per-token cost basis is identical down to the lamport. Whales cannot frontrun retail.
