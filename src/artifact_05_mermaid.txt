    U[Participant Wallet] -->|Deposit SOL| W[Solana Wallet Adapter]
    W -->|deposit instruction| P[$CHIMP Fair Launch Program]

    P --> L[Launch PDA]
    P --> PA[Participant PDA]
    P --> SV[Program-Derived SOL Vault]

    ADMIN[Launch Authority] -->|create / activate / close / finalize| P

    P -->|state transition event| RPC[Solana RPC]
    RPC --> IDX[Indexer / Automation Worker]

    IDX --> DB[(Optional PostgreSQL)]
    IDX --> API[Read API / Health Endpoint]
    IDX --> RESEND[Resend Email API]
    IDX --> SLACK[Slack/Discord Webhook]
    IDX --> OBS[Structured Logs / Metrics]

    P -->|finalization proof| EXPLORER[Solana Explorer]
    U -->|independent verification| EXPLORER
